Our Impact Framework

Six Pillars of Community Impact

Every initiative at The Paws is rooted in one of six transformational pillars. Together they form a holistic model that turns a football pitch into a launchpad for life.

01
⚽
Football Excellence

World-class technical training delivered by dedicated coaches, giving every young athlete — regardless of economic background — the tools to compete at the highest level.

Structured training sessions 5 days a week for all age groups
Participation in Kajiado County football leagues and regional tournaments
Talent pathway towards professional clubs and national teams
KU TV media coverage recognising youth football development
300+
Active Players
02
📚
Academic Support

We believe that the classroom and the pitch are equal arenas. Our academic programme ensures no child must choose between sport and education.

Homework support and tutoring sessions integrated into weekly schedule
Scholarship pathways identified and actively pursued for promising athletes
Partnerships with local schools to track academic progress
Reading and numeracy catch-up programmes for younger players
100%
School Enrolment
03
🧭
Life Skills & Mentorship

Football teaches discipline, resilience and teamwork. We extend those lessons off the pitch through dedicated workshops, mentorship sessions and leadership circles.

Monthly life-skills workshops covering communication, finance and health
Mentorship pairing older players with younger members as role models
Leadership development programme for teen captains and coaches
Mental wellness awareness sessions embedded in training calendar
12+
Workshops / Year
04
🌿
Environmental Stewardship

Sitting on the edge of Nairobi National Park, we carry a unique responsibility. We nurture a generation of environmental guardians who respect and protect the wild world around them.

Annual tree-planting drives in and around the Kajiado corridor
Awareness programmes on human-wildlife coexistence near Nairobi NP
Zero-waste policy at all training sessions and events
Youth-led community clean-up campaigns in Ongata Rongai
500+
Trees Planted
05
🤝
Gender Inclusion

Equal access, equal opportunity. From our founding day, girls and boys have shared the same pitches, the same coaches and the same dreams. No exceptions.

Dedicated girls' teams at all age categories, fully resourced and coached
Female coaching appointments championed and prioritised
Anti-harassment and safeguarding policies actively enforced
Girls' participation rates tracked and reported publicly each season
50/50
Boys / Girls

Over 20 trained volunteer coaches and mentors from the local community
Open-door policy — every child from any background is welcome, free of charge
Parent and guardian engagement integrated into the academy calendar
Partnerships with local health clinics for youth wellness screenings

Our Journey

From 2019 to Today

Every step forward has been driven by community, passion, and the belief that football can change lives.

2019
Foundation Year
The Paws Are Born

Diaspora Soccer Academy is established in Ongata Rongai by passionate community members. Named after the wildlife of Nairobi National Park, the academy opens its doors to its first cohort of young players.

2020
Growth Under Adversity
Community Holds Strong

Despite global challenges, volunteer coaches keep the programme alive. Life skills workshops and academic support sessions are launched to help youth through uncertain times.

2021
Formal Structure
Official Registration & Six Pillars Defined

The academy formalises its structure, with the six community pillars established as its operational framework. Girls' teams are launched across all age groups. Over 150 youth enrolled.

2022
Environmental Action
Green Programme Launched

The environmental stewardship pillar goes active with tree-planting drives and clean-up campaigns around Kajiado County. Nairobi National Park partners consulted for habitat awareness education.

2023
Media Recognition
KU TV Spotlight

Kenya's KU TV features Diaspora Soccer Academy, bringing national recognition to The Paws' story. Player count surpasses 250. First scholarship pathway confirmed for a senior player.

NOW
Present Day
300+ Youth, Six Pillars, One Community

The Paws serve over 300 young people aged 5–23 across Kajiado. All six pillars are fully active. The academy actively seeks partners, sponsors and donors to scale its impact across Kenya.

Opportunities Ahead

Recommended Next Steps

Strategic additions that could amplify the academy's six-pillar impact and build long-term sustainability.

🤝
Corporate Partnerships

Formalise a CSR partnership programme with Nairobi-based companies. Offer branded kit, pitch signage and impact reports in exchange for funding — a win for sponsors and The Paws.

📱
Digital Storytelling Hub

Create a dedicated social media content calendar documenting each pillar monthly. Player spotlights, coach interviews and environmental updates build donor confidence and community pride.

🎓
Scholarship Fund

Launch a named scholarship fund — "The Paws Scholarship" — targeting university entry for graduating athletes. Even one success story per year redefines community aspirations.

🌍
Diaspora Engagement Network

Tap the Kenyan diaspora globally for mentors, fundraising and professional connections. A diaspora ambassador programme would deepen the academy's identity and resource base.

📊
Annual Impact Report

Publish a professional annual report mapping progress across all six pillars with data, stories and photography. This signals accountability and professionalism to donors and grant bodies.

🏟️
Permanent Training Ground

Explore land acquisition or long-term lease to secure a permanent, branded home ground. A fixed address dramatically increases institutional credibility and creates a community anchor.
